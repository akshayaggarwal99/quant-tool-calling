Background. Language models run on laptops after post-training quantization, and the format that ships is the llama.cpp k-quant. The accuracy cost of that compression has been measured on prose, knowledge and reasoning benchmarks, and not on the output an agent produces, which is a function call that a program then executes. Methods. We built a ﬁve-rung k-quant ladder, Q8_0 to Q3_K_M, from one FP16 source for Qwen3-1.7B, and three-rung ladders for Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-14B, Llama-3.2-3B and Llama-3.1-8B. Every rung was scored on the Berkeley Function Calling Leaderboard’s simple_python category by deterministic syntax-tree matching (n = 400 or 200), with a type-coerced secondary score, and on 400 GSM8K questions with the same weight ﬁles. Rungs are compared with McNemar’s exact test on identical items, Holm-corrected within each model; every accuracy and paired difference carries a 95% interval; two rungs were run three times to measure the instrument. Five hypotheses with thresholds were written in a dated ﬁle on the ﬁrst day; its ﬁrst commit postdates the ﬁrst ladder, so the study is not called pre-registered, and every departure from the ﬁle is tabulated. Results. On Qwen3-1.7B tool calling is ﬂat from Q8_0 through Q4_K_M and loses 4.75 points at Q3_K_M (Holm-adjusted p = 0.017), about 10 times the run-to-run range. Free-form mathematics on those ﬁles loses 39.50 points at that rung because 213 of 400 generations reach the 4,096-token budget while still reasoning; 29 of them commit to a correct answer ﬁrst. Qwen3-8B and Qwen3-14B show no loss at any rung. Llama-3.2-3B loses 5.50 strict points at Q3_K_M (Holm p = 0.030) and a suggestive 4.00 at Q4_K_M (p = 0.021, Holm 0.064, one run); under the coerced verdict neither loss is detectable. Llama-3.1-8B’s strict score rises from 50.00% to 71.50% down the ladder because its full-precision weights write numbers as quoted strings; scored on the parsed value, with 13 of its 14 three-bit decode failures re-read as the well-formed JSON calls they are, it scores 93.00%, 93.00% and 91.50%. A logit penalty on overthinking markers changes tool-calling accuracy by 0.00 points. The multi-turn arm is confounded: 46, 59 and 137 of 200 trajectories aborted on a server context limit shared by four slots. Conclusions. At four bits no model shows a single-call loss larger than its paired interval allows: under the coerced verdict no lower interval end is below −5.2 points and the minimum detectable difference is 2.8 to 3.7 points. At three bits Qwen3-1.7B and, under strict scoring, Llama-3.2-3B lose accuracy; the other three models do not detectably. The length-driven collapse of chain-of-thought reasoning under three-bit weights does not reach a single tool call. A strict schema score can move in either direction under quantization because it also measures a formatting habit, and part of a checker’s decode-failure count can be its own parser, so a coerced score and the failure types belong beside it. 40
